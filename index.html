Three powertrains

Every 2026 Grand Highlander is the same size inside and out — 201.4 in. long, 116.1 in. wheelbase, 97.5 cu. ft. behind the front seats. The powertrain decides how it drives, how much it tows, and what it costs to fuel.

Reading the three side by side. The 2.5L Hybrid is the efficiency choice — the same 34 mpg combined on XLE, Limited and Nightshade. Hybrid MAX is the performance choice: 362 hp, 400 lb.-ft., a conventional 6-speed with paddle shifters, full-time AWD and 5,000 lb. towing, at 27 mpg combined. Gas gives the same 5,000 lb. towing with an 8-speed, and it's the only way to get a Platinum without Hybrid MAX.

Seating & space

Seven or eight seats

  • 7-passenger — second-row fold-down captain's chairs with fold-down inboard armrests, adjustable headrests and two cup holders mounted on the floor; 60/40 split, fold-down third-row seats with sliding headrests and recline function. Standard on XLE, Hybrid XLE, Limited, Hybrid Limited, Hybrid Nightshade, Hybrid MAX Limited, Platinum, Hybrid MAX Platinum.
  • 8-passenger — 60/40 split, sliding and reclining fold-down second-row bench seats. Standard on LE and Hybrid LE; a no-cost factory option (Bench Seat, code BS) on XLE, Hybrid XLE, Limited and Hybrid Limited. Not offered on Nightshade, Hybrid MAX Limited, Platinum or Hybrid MAX Platinum.
  • LATCH lower anchors and tethers on outboard second-row seats and tether anchor on third-row seats
